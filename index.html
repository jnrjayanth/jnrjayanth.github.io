What I do

Measure before you ship

Most of my work sits where a model meets a task nobody has tested it on yet. That means building the harness, curating the data, designing the prompt, running inference across commercial APIs and self-hosted open-source models, and reporting the numbers plainly.

I work in Python across PyTorch and Hugging Face, and with the OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google APIs. I'm equally comfortable moving a workload onto a local GPU when cost, latency, or privacy makes that the better call.

Selected work

Can a dashcam see it coming?

Millions of cars have no collision warning system but already carry a dashcam. My thesis asks whether a general-purpose vision-language model, with no task-specific training, can look at twelve frames of footage and tell you a crash is about to happen.

I built a 1,960-clip benchmark spanning safe urban driving, ego-involved crashes, animal encounters, and pedestrian incidents, then ran eight models against it under identical zero-shot conditions. GPT-4o led on accuracy at 82.3% — but accuracy turned out to be the wrong thing to rank on.
